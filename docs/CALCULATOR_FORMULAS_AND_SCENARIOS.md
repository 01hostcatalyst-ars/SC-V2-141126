Core Mathematical FormulasStep 1: Continuous 16-Knot Piecewise Linear Base Rate InterpolationFor a creator with follower or subscriber count $F$ falling between adjacent market knots $(F_k, P_k)$ and $(F_{k+1}, P_{k+1})$:  $$R_{\text{base}}(F) = P_k + \left( \frac{F - F_k}{F_{k+1} - F_k} \right) \cdot (P_{k+1} - P_k) \quad \text{[cite: 3]}$$Simultaneously, the target benchmark engagement rate $\text{ER}_{\text{target}}$ interpolates across the identical bracket to eliminate step-cliff penalties:$$\text{ER}_{\text{target}}(F) = \text{ER}_k + \left( \frac{F - F_k}{F_{k+1} - F_k} \right) \cdot (\text{ER}_{k+1} - \text{ER}_k)$$Step 2: Platform-Specific Engagement & Performance Multipliers ($M_{\text{perf}}$)Evaluates creator interaction against continuous empirical platform baselines, bounded between $0.70\times$ (floor) and $1.40\times$ (ceiling):$$M_{\text{perf}} = \text{clamp}\big(1.0 + \text{ER Log Factor} + \text{View Log Factor}, \; 0.70, \; 1.40\big)$$1. Instagram Interaction FormulaBecause video view counts are deprecated across public Instagram interfaces, Instagram evaluates interaction strength against follower count:  $$\text{ER}_{\text{Instagram}} = \left( \frac{\text{Median Likes} + \text{Median Comments}}{\text{Follower Count}} \right) \times 100\% \quad \text{[cite: 1, 4]}$$$$\text{ER Log Factor} = 0.15 \cdot \log_2 \left( \frac{\text{Observed ER}_{\text{Instagram}}}{\text{ER}_{\text{target}}(F)} \right)$$$$\text{View Log Factor} = 0.00 \quad (\text{Neutral fallback for unauthenticated public scraping})$$2. TikTok Interaction FormulaEvaluated against median video play counts:  $$\text{ER}_{\text{TikTok}} = \left( \frac{\text{Median Likes} + \text{Median Comments} + \text{Median Shares}}{\text{Median Video Views}} \right) \times 100\% \quad \text{[cite: 4]}$$$$\text{ER Log Factor} = 0.15 \cdot \log_2 \left( \frac{\text{Observed ER}_{\text{TikTok}}}{\text{ER}_{\text{target}}(F)} \right)$$$$\text{View Log Factor} = 0.15 \cdot \log_2 \left( \frac{\text{Observed Median Views}}{\text{Expected Views}(F)} \right)$$3. YouTube Long-Form Interaction Formula (Track A)Evaluates dedicated reviews and 60–90s integrations strictly against long-form median viewership:$$\text{ER}_{\text{long}} = \left( \frac{\text{Median Likes}_{\text{long}} + \text{Median Comments}_{\text{long}}}{\text{Median Views}_{\text{long}}} \right) \times 100\%$$$$\text{ER Log Factor}_{\text{long}} = 0.15 \cdot \log_2 \left( \frac{\text{Observed ER}_{\text{long}}}{\text{ER}_{\text{target, long}}(F)} \right)$$$$\text{View Log Factor}_{\text{long}} = 0.15 \cdot \log_2 \left( \frac{\text{Observed Long-Form Views}}{\text{Expected Long-Form Views}(F)} \right)$$4. YouTube Shorts Interaction Formula (Track B)Evaluates vertical short-form content strictly against Shorts viewership:$$\text{ER}_{\text{short}} = \left( \frac{\text{Median Likes}_{\text{short}} + \text{Median Comments}_{\text{short}}}{\text{Median Views}_{\text{short}}} \right) \times 100\%$$$$\text{ER Log Factor}_{\text{short}} = 0.15 \cdot \log_2 \left( \frac{\text{Observed ER}_{\text{short}}}{4.50\%} \right)$$$$\text{View Log Factor}_{\text{short}} = 0.15 \cdot \log_2 \left( \frac{\text{Observed Shorts Views}}{\text{Expected Shorts Views}(F)} \right)$$Step 3: Sourced Niche Multiplier Index ($I_{\text{niche}}$)Ratios are calculated against Aspire's verified Lifestyle baseline median of $\$562$:  $$I_{\text{niche}} = \frac{\text{Category Median Reel Rate}}{\$562 \text{ (Aspire Lifestyle Baseline)}} \quad \text{[cite: 3]}$$Step 4: Damped Fee Calculation & Anti-Runaway CapFor each deliverable asset in the requested scope:$$\text{Deliverable Fee} = \min\Big(R_{\text{base}} \times I_{\text{niche}} \times M_{\text{perf}}, \; R_{\text{base}} \times 2.25\Big)$$$$\text{Gross Deliverables Total} = \sum_{i=1}^{n} \text{Deliverable Fee}_i$$Step 5: Multi-Asset Bundle Volume Discount ($D_{\text{bundle}}$)Applies tiered scope discounts to multi-deliverable packages:  $$D_{\text{bundle}} = \begin{cases} 0\%, & N_{\text{assets}} = 1 \text{[cite: 3]} \\ 10\%, & 2 \le N_{\text{assets}} \le 3 \text{[cite: 3]} \\ 15\%, & N_{\text{assets}} \ge 4 \text{[cite: 3]} \end{cases}$$$$\text{Net Content Fee} = \text{Gross Deliverables Total} \times (1 - D_{\text{bundle}})$$Step 6: Additive Commercial Rights & Exclusivity SurchargesCommercial terms scale additively on top of the discounted Net Content Fee:  $$\text{Commercial Rights Fee} = \text{Net Content Fee} \times (P_{\text{usage}} + P_{\text{exclusivity}} + P_{\text{whitelisting}})$$$P_{\text{usage}}$ = Paid Digital Usage Surcharge (e.g., $+25\%$ for 30-day ads).  $P_{\text{exclusivity}}$ = Category Lockout Surcharge (e.g., $+25\%$ for 2 weeks).  $P_{\text{whitelisting}}$ = Creator Ad Handle Permission Surcharge ($+30\%$).  Step 7: Production Addons & Turnaround Fees$$\text{Total Production Addons} = (\text{Net Content Fee} \times P_{\text{rush}}) + \sum \text{Flat Addons}$$Expedited Turnaround (72h): $+25\%$ of Net Content FeeRush Turnaround (48h): $+35\%$ of Net Content FeeUltra Rush Turnaround (24h): $+50\%$ of Net Content FeeRaw B-Roll Footage Buyout: $+30\%$ of Net Content FeeAdditional Round of Revisions: $\$150$ flat per revisionLink-in-Bio Placement: $\$200$ flat per 30 daysPinned Profile Highlight: $\$100$ flat per 30 daysStep 8: Global GDP Purchasing Power Parity (PPP) & FX Calibration$$\text{Total Global USD} = \text{Net Content Fee} + \text{Commercial Rights Fee} + \text{Total Production Addons}$$$$\text{Localized Target Median} = \text{Total Global USD} \times \text{PPP Index}_{\text{Country}} \times \text{FX Rate}_{\text{Country}}$$Step 9: Defended Negotiation Bounds$$\text{Budget Offer (Negotiation Floor)} = \text{Localized Target Median} \times 0.80$$$$\text{Typical Valuation (Defended Median)} = \mathbf{\text{Localized Target Median} \times 1.00}$$$$\text{Premium Ask (High-Demand / Agency)} = \text{Localized Target Median} \times 1.25$$3.
